Practice for SolidWorks certification

The Learn tab has original practice parts in the style of SolidWorks' certification exams, with a drawing, a timer and answers checked against your model, plus quick quizzes for the other certifications.

Which exams

Dassault Systèmes runs the SolidWorks certification program. The practice parts follow the format of three of its exams:

ExamWhat it isPractice parts
CSWA, Certified SOLIDWORKS AssociateThe entry-level exam. Model a part from a drawing, make design changes, and report the mass.3
CSWP, Certified SOLIDWORKS ProfessionalThe core professional exam. Busier parts, several rounds of design changes, mass and center of mass.7
CSWPA-SM, Sheet MetalOne of the advanced specialist exams. Formed sheet metal parts, with the mass and the flat pattern length.2

Why only Sheet Metal of the advanced exams? A practice part is only useful if its answer is exact, and Caddie computes every answer key from the part's geometry. It can do that for formed sheet metal, but not yet for weldments, surfacing or mold tooling, so those topics are covered by quiz questions instead.

Where the parts come from. They're original, written for Caddie in the style of the exams, not copies of exam questions.

How a practice part works

  1. Open the Learn tab and pick a part under Practice exams. Each has a drawing, the values for its lettered dimensions, the units, the material and the origin, and three questions.
  2. Model it in a new SolidWorks part, with the units and material given.
  3. Answer. Type the number and click Check, or click Use my model and Caddie reads the mass, volume or center of mass straight from your part.
  4. If it's wrong, click Hint. It tells you what's off without the answer, such as "Your part has exactly the extra material that the two base holes should remove."
  5. Next question. Later questions change a dimension ("Change A (width) to 70"), as the exams do, so you edit your model instead of starting again.

The parts

TrackPartsTime
CSWA styleMounting plate, Step block, Slotted link8 to 10 minutes
CSWP styleBolted flange, Angle bracket, Pillow block, Keyed hub, Pocketed cover, Rocker arm, Clevis12 to 15 minutes
Sheet Metal styleSheet-metal angle, Sheet-metal channel (including the flat pattern length)12 minutes

Dimensions are chosen from realistic values each time, so you can practice the same part again. Every answer key was checked by building the part in SolidWorks twice, at two different sets of sizes: 24 builds, all within 0.003% on volume.

Exam conditions

Turn on Exam conditions at the top of Practice exams to practice like the real thing: hints and Ask Caddie are hidden, answers are saved without being checked, and you see your results at the end.

When you're stuck

  • Hint says what's off, not the answer.
  • Ask Caddie sends the problem, your answer and your model to Caddie, which coaches you through it.
  • Show answer gives you the key when you want to move on.
  • Build the answer builds the correct part in a new SolidWorks document, so you can compare it with yours feature by feature.

Conversations on this page are word for word from Caddie's test runs on sample models.

Mounting plate (CSWA style) · Part1, one corner hole missing
You
My mass doesn't match the expected answer. What's wrong with my model?
Caddie
Sketch2 appears to be missing one corner hole. It has four sketch segments, but the plate needs five circles: four corner holes and one center hole. The off-center mass also points to a missing corner hole.
Mounting plate (CSWA style) · Part1 in Plain Carbon Steel
You
My mass is far higher than the expected answer. What did I miss?
Caddie
Your material is the issue: Part1 is assigned Plain Carbon Steel instead of the specified 6061 Alloy. Steel is much denser, and your model’s volume matches the practice part, so the geometry isn’t what’s driving the high mass.

About the real exams

  • Practice parts are original, in the style of the exams, not copies of real exam questions.
  • Caddie won't help during a real certification exam or any graded test, even if asked. The exam has to be your own work.
  • Exams are run by Dassault Systèmes' certification program, and Caddie isn't affiliated with it. Check its site for current formats, times and passing scores.
