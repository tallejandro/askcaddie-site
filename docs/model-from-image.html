Model from a drawing, sketch or photo

Give Caddie a picture of what you want to make. It reads the shape and the dimensions, tells you what it assumed, and builds it with you.

Conversations on this page are word for word from Caddie's test runs on sample models.

Attach an image

  • Paste a screenshot into the chat box with Ctrl+V,
  • drag an image file onto the panel, or
  • click the paperclip next to the box and choose a file.

Add a line about what you want, or just send it. Large images are shrunk to 1600 pixels before they're sent.

A real example

This drawing was attached to an empty part with the message "Model this bracket from the attached drawing."

Caddie proposed the whole part as one set of changes for a single approval:

  1. "Build the 80 mm wide aluminium angle bracket with its R8 inside corner and three through-holes, assuming the undimensioned hole positions are symmetric about the width." A seven-entity side profile on the Right Plane and an 80 mm extrude, a sketch and a through-all cut for the two base holes, another for the upright hole, and 6061 Alloy.
Part1 · after the change
You
Model this bracket from the attached drawing.
Caddie

The bracket is set up with the R8 inside corner, three through-holes and 6061 aluminium.

I assumed the base holes are symmetric and the upright hole is centered across the width, since those positions aren’t dimensioned.

What works best

  • Dimensioned drawings: Caddie uses the numbers as given, including the material and units if they're on the drawing.
  • Hand sketches: write the dimensions on them. Caddie asks about anything it can't read.
  • Photos of real parts: include one known size, such as the overall length or a hole diameter, so Caddie can scale the rest. Expect a question or two.
  • Screenshots of other CAD software or of a PDF drawing work the same way.

Prefer to model it yourself?

Ask Caddie to guide you through modelling it and it starts a guide instead, with each step highlighted. To practise with answers checked, use the parts on the Learn tab.

Good to know

  • Caddie builds with sketches, extrudes, cuts, fillets, chamfers, shells, mirrors and patterns. For revolves, sweeps and lofts it guides you through the steps instead.
  • It can misread a dimension or a view. Check the assumptions it lists, and measure the result.
  • Attached images are sent to OpenAI so they can be read. Don't attach anything you aren't allowed to share. See Privacy and data.
