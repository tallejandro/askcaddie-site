Sheet metal, weldments and holes

Caddie reads what the shop cares about: sheet metal thickness and bend settings, weldment cut lists, and the size and depth of every Hole Wizard hole.

Conversations on this page are word for word from Caddie's test runs on sample models.

Sheet metal

For a sheet metal part, Caddie reads the thickness, inside bend radius, bend allowance (K-factor, bend allowance, bend deduction or a table), the relief ratio, whether the part is flattened, and the flat pattern's size and bend count from the cut list.

Cover.SLDPRT · sheet metal, 1.5 mm AISI 304
You
What K-factor is this part using?
Caddie
The part is using a K-factor of 0.44.
Cover.SLDPRT · sheet metal, 1.5 mm AISI 304
You
What blank size do I need for this part?
Caddie

The flat blank is 182.6 × 95 mm in 1.5 mm AISI 304 sheet.

That size uses the model’s K-factor of 0.44, so confirm the bend allowance with your fabricator before cutting.

Weldments

For a weldment, Caddie reads the cut list: each profile's description, length, end angles and quantity.

Frame.SLDPRT · weldment
You
What's the cut list for the square tubing?
Caddie

The square tubing cut list for 30 × 30 × 2.60 mm tube is:

  • 4 pieces at 630 mm, with 45° cuts at both ends
  • 4 pieces at 970 mm, with square cuts at both ends
Frame.SLDPRT · weldment
You
Which of the tubes have mitred ends?
Caddie
The four 630 mm lengths of 30 × 30 × 2.60 mm square tube have 45° mitres at both ends. The four 970 mm tubes have square ends.

Hole Wizard holes

For each Hole Wizard feature, Caddie reads the hole type and standard, the size and fastener, whether it's through or blind, its depth, counterbore or countersink sizes, thread depth, and how many holes there are. Ask what size the tapped holes are, and it answers with the thread, the thread depth and the drill depth from the feature itself.

Questions to try

What blank size do I need?How many bends are there, and what's the inside radius?Give me a cut list for the saw.Which fasteners go in these holes?Is the counterbore deep enough for an M8 cap screw?Why is the flat pattern larger than expected?
Check with your fabricator. Bend allowances depend on the tooling and material at the shop. Caddie reads what's in the model, which is only as right as the settings in it.
